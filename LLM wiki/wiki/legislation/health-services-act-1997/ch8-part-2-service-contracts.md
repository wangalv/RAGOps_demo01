Classifies [[service-contract]] types (Division 1), governs their formation (Division 2), and
establishes a specialised arbitration regime before the Industrial Relations Commission for
system-wide fee-for-service/sessional contract disputes (Division 3).

## Sections

| s. | Heading | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| 80 | What is a service contract? | Agreement between a public health organisation and a practitioner (or their practice company) for specified medical services; excludes tendering-process arrangements. |
| 81 | Kinds of service contracts | Fee-for-service, sessional, honorary (non-exhaustive). |
| 82 | Fee-for-service contract | Remunerated by a fee scale for kinds of service. |
| 83 | Sessional contract | Remunerated by hourly rate(s), not fee-for-service. |
| 84 | Honorary contract | Not for monetary remuneration. |
| 85 | Electing to provide services via practice company | Practitioner may elect a practice-company contract if the company carries public liability and professional indemnity insurance. |
| 86 | Service contracts to be in writing | VMO/honorary medical officer appointment void unless in a written service contract. |
| 87 | Minister may approve standard conditions | Minister may approve [[australian-medical-association-nsw]]-recommended condition sets by order; defines [[standard-service-contract]]; order must specify a ≤5-year period for s.89(3). |
| 88 | Standard service contracts to be used | Must use the standard contract's conditions where one exists (doesn't apply to honorary contracts); contravening contract is void. |
| 89 | Application for appointment of arbitrator | Minister or the Association may apply to the [[relevant-minister]] for an arbitrator on fee-for-service/sessional contract terms system-wide. |
| 90 | Appointment of arbitrator | Relevant Minister appoints a nominated Industrial Relations Commission judicial member. |
| 91 | Nature of determination | Arbitrator determines terms/conditions/remuneration and effective dates; must try to bring parties to agreement. |
| 92 | Manner of exercise of arbitrator's functions | Not bound by evidence rules; must act judicially, with equity/good conscience; must have regard to IRC principles and the public interest (economy, fiscal position). |
| 93 | Rights of appearance, oaths, legal representation | Minister/Association appear as of right; others need leave and a special interest; arbitrator may administer oaths; legal representation permitted. |
| 94 | Conduct of proceedings and arbitrator protection | Arbitrator determines procedure; may sit in public/private; has Supreme Court judge-level protection/immunity. |
| 95 | Notification and finality of determination | Written notice to Minister/Association; determination is final and unreviewable except via s.96 appeal. |
| 96 | Appeal to Full Bench | Minister or Association may appeal to the Full Bench of the IRC. |
| 97 | Interpretation of determination | Minister or Association may seek a declaration of right from the IRC in Court Session. |
| 98 | Determination contractually binding | Inconsistent service contract terms are overridden by the determination from its effective date. |

## Key obligations and powers

- **Void-ing conditions** (s.86, s.88(2)) — non-written appointments, and contracts departing
  from a mandatory standard contract, are void.
- **Power** (s.87) — Minister approves standard condition sets on Association recommendation.
- **Power** (s.89, s.90) — Minister/Association may trigger binding arbitration; relevant Minister
  appoints the arbitrator (an IRC judicial member).
- **Obligation** (s.91(2)) — arbitrator must endeavour to bring parties to agreement.
- **Obligation** (s.92(2)) — arbitrator must have regard to IRC principles and public-interest
  factors (economy, fiscal position).
