Recognises non-government (often religious/charitable) organisations as part of the public health
system in respect of specific listed establishments/services, and sets out their by-law,
transfer, and liability rules. No Parts — flat Chapter.

## Sections

| s. | Heading | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| 62 | Recognition of affiliated health organisations | Org listed in Schedule 3 col 1 is an AHO re its Schedule 3 col 2 establishments/services; Governor may amend Schedule 3, but only with the organisation's consent. |
| 62A | Declared affiliated health organisations | Regulations may declare an AHO "declared" (can't employ staff directly, relies on [[nsw-health-service]]); needs the AHO's concurrence. |
| 62B | Recognition of networked AHOs | Minister may declare AHOs a "network" for National Health Reform Agreement purposes, on application or own motion, with AHO consent. |
| 63 | AHOs may make by-laws | With Minister approval, on a similar list to s.39/s.60 (management, patient services, visiting practitioners, staff discipline, records, meetings); visiting-practitioner by-laws need Medical Services Committee advice. |
| 63A | Criminal/disciplinary matters for non-declared AHO employees | Ch.9 Pt 2 (staff misconduct reporting regime) applied with modifications to non-declared AHOs and their own employees. |
| 64 | Transfer of recognised establishments/services | Governor may transfer to a LHD/statutory health corporation by order, but only with the AHO's consent. |
| 64A | Regulations on staff movement | Regulations may govern movement of staff between AHOs and the NSW Health Service (not declared AHOs); Minister may direct on liability adjustments. |
| 65 | Minister may determine role/functions/activities | Minister determines and directs, after consulting the AHO having regard to its health care philosophy. |
| 66 | Appointment of chief executives | A person can't be appointed CE of an AHO's recognised establishment/service without Health Secretary consent. |
| 67 | Liability of AHOs | Acts done in good faith by the org/governing body/members/agents don't attract personal liability. |

## Key obligations and powers

- **Consent requirement running throughout**: ss.62(4), 62A(5), 62B(5), 64(2) — the Crown cannot
  unilaterally recognise, declare, network, or transfer an AHO's establishments without its
  consent. This is the Chapter's defining feature vs. LHDs/statutory health corporations, which
  the Crown controls more directly.
- **Obligation** (s.65(2)) — Minister must consult the AHO, having regard to its health care
  philosophy, before determining role/functions/activities.
- **Precondition** (s.66) — AHO establishment/service CE appointment needs Health Secretary
  consent.
