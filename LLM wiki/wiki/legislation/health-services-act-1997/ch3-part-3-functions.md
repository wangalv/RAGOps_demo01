The operational powers of a local health district: joint management arrangements, opening/closing
services, property, contracts, investments, by-laws, and delegation.

## Sections

| s. | Heading | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| 30 | Combined management/assistance | LHDs may jointly manage, or manage for another LHD/non-district health organisation, with Minister approval required to enter the agreement. |
| 31 | Opening and closing of services | LHD may establish/close/restrict services; must notify [[health-secretary]] and ensure the decision fits its functions before implementing. |
| 32 | Determination of role, functions, activities | Health Secretary may determine role/functions/activities and direct the LHD; Minister may direct establishment/closure/restriction in the public interest. |
| 33 | (Repealed) | — |
| 34 | Powers in relation to property | May acquire/dispose/dedicate land; acquiring or disposing land requires Minister approval. |
| 35 | Application of Public Works Act 1912 | Land acquisitions under s.34 treated as authorised works under that Act. |
| 36 | Power to accept gifts, devises, bequests | May acquire property by gift if consistent with its functions; may act as trustee. |
| 37 | Contracts of local health district | May contract for services/goods for its functions; contracting to provide services to others needs Health Secretary approval. |
| 38 | Investments | May invest money held, per GSF Act Pt 6 or Minister/Treasurer-approved means. |
| 39 | Local health district by-laws | Health Secretary may make model by-laws (hospital management, fees, visiting practitioners, smoke-free areas, records, committees); LHD adopts/modifies with Health Secretary approval; visiting-practitioner by-laws need Medical Services Committee advice first. |
| 40 | Delegations by local health district | May delegate functions to NSW Health Service members, visiting practitioners/councils/committees, Minister/Health Secretary-appointed bodies, or prescribed persons — except the power of delegation itself, s.31(2) functions, or by-law making. |

## Key obligations and powers

- **Obligation** (s.31(3)) — must notify Health Secretary and check fit-with-functions before
  implementing an opening/closing decision.
- **Obligation** (s.34(2)) — must not acquire/dispose/dedicate land without Minister approval.
- **Obligation** (s.37(2)) — needs Health Secretary approval to contract to provide services to
  others.
- **Power** (s.32(2)) — Minister may direct a LHD to establish/close/restrict services in the
  public interest.
- **Power** (s.40) — LHD may delegate functions, subject to carve-outs.
- **Precondition** (s.39(6)) — visiting-practitioner by-laws require Medical Services Committee
  advice (with a 30-day default-through exception at s.39(7)).
