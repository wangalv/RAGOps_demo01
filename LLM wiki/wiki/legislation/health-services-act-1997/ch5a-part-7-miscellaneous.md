Closing provisions for the ambulance fee regime: write-offs and service of notices.

## Sections

| s. | Heading | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| 67ZE | Unpaid fees may be written off | [[health-secretary]] may write off unpaid fees, in whole or part; if already referred for debt recovery, only after the referral is revoked or action suspended/cancelled. |
| 67ZF | Service of notices | Fee invoices/debt notices/outcome notices served personally or by post; other notices also by electronic transmission (with consent); postal service presumed effective after 7 days unless rebutted. |
| 67ZG | Form of notices to Chief Commissioner | May be given by post, document exchange, or electronic transmission. |

## Key obligations and powers

- **Power** (s.67ZE) — Health Secretary may write off unpaid fees, with a referral-status
  precondition.
- **Restriction** (s.67ZF(3)) — electronic service requires the person's consent.
