A single section re-adopting the Commonwealth's Medicare Principles and Commitments as NSW
guidelines — a condition of Commonwealth financial assistance for public hospital services.

## Sections

| s. | Heading | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| 68 | Medicare Principles and Commitments | Adopts, as guidelines (not enforceable civil rights — s.68(4)), 3 Principles (free choice of public treatment; access on clinical need; equitable provision regardless of location) and 2 Commitments (public information; efficiency/quality improvement), text drawn from s.26 of the Commonwealth *Health Insurance Act 1973*. |

## Key obligations and powers

None enforceable — s.68(4) expressly excludes civil causes of action and new legal rights arising
from this section. Guidelines, not obligations.
