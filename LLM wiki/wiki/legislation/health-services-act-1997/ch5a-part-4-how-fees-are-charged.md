The procedural mechanics of billing: fee invoices, escalation to debt notices, required content,
minimum notice periods, and changing payment arrangements.

## Sections

| s. | Heading | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| 67P | Fee invoices | [[health-secretary]] charges by issuing and serving a fee invoice; must specify amount, services, due date, fee-review info, and any regulation-required content. |
| 67Q | Debt notice | If unpaid 7+ days after due date, Health Secretary may issue a debt notice; must include debt-recovery-referral warning and fee-review info; declared a debt notice for State Debt Recovery Act 2018 purposes. |
| 67R | Required information about fee reviews | Specifies the mandatory content of fee-review advice included in invoices/debt notices. |
| 67S | Due date for payment | Must be ≥21 days after service; postal service defects are cured by extending to 21 days from posting. |
| 67T | Change of payment arrangements | Health Secretary may reduce, extend time for, or permit instalments on a fee, per the payment rules; cannot change arrangements after referral to the Chief Commissioner unless the referral is revoked. |

## Key obligations and powers

- **Obligation** (s.67P(2), s.67Q(2)) — invoices/debt notices must contain specified content.
- **Obligation** (s.67S(1)) — due date must be at least 21 days after service.
- **Restriction** (s.67T(5)) — Health Secretary must not change payment arrangements after
  referral to the Chief Commissioner unless the referral is revoked.
