Mirrors [[ch3-part-3-functions]] for statutory health corporations, with the added complexity of
a "relevant authority" that varies by governance type (Minister for board-governed; Health
Secretary for chief-executive- and specialty-network-governed).

## Sections

| s. | Heading | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| 53A | Combined management/assistance | Corporations may jointly manage or manage for another, with Minister approval to enter the agreement. |
| 53 | Determination of functions | "Relevant authority" determines role/functions/activities and may direct; Minister may direct establishment/closure/restriction in the public interest. |
| 54 | Other committees and councils | CE may establish committees/councils. |
| 55 | Powers in relation to property | Acquire/dispose/dedicate land; needs Minister approval. |
| 56 | Application of Public Works Act 1912 | Land acquisitions treated as authorised works. |
| 57 | Power to accept gifts, devises, bequests | May acquire property by gift; may act as trustee. |
| 58 | Contracts | May contract for its functions; contracting to serve others needs relevant authority's approval. |
| 59 | Investments | May invest per GSF Act Pt 6 or Minister/Treasurer-approved means. |
| 60 | By-laws | Relevant authority may make model by-laws (parallel list to s.39, plus board-governed-specific procedure by-laws); visiting-practitioner by-laws need Medical Services Committee advice. |
| 61 | Delegations | May delegate to NSW Health Service members, except delegation power itself or by-law making; relevant authority may direct on delegations. |

## Key obligations and powers

- **Defined term used throughout**: "relevant authority" = Minister (board governed) or Health
  Secretary (chief-executive/specialty-network governed) — defined per-section (s.53(3), s.58(4),
  s.60(11), s.61(5)), not in the Dictionary.
- **Obligation** (s.55(2)) — needs Minister approval to acquire/dispose/dedicate land.
- **Precondition** (s.60(6)) — visiting-practitioner by-laws need Medical Services Committee
  advice first.
