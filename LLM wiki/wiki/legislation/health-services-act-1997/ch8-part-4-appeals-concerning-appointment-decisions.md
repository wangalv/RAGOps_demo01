The appeal pathway for a [[visiting-practitioner]] whose privileges are reduced or appointment is
not renewed/suspended/terminated: notice, appeal to the Minister, and determination by a
[[committee-of-review]].

## Sections

| s. | Heading | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| 105 | Notification of certain decisions | Org must give written notice + reasons within 14 days of reducing privileges, not re-appointing, or suspending/terminating. |
| 106 | Right of appeal to Minister | Person may appeal to the [[minister]]; carve-outs for cessation-of-appointment-type, competence-based reductions, and (mirroring s.103) serious sex/violence offence terminations against minors/patients. |
| 107 | Notice of appeal | Appellant must give notice of grounds within 1 month (or a longer Minister-allowed period). |
| 108 | Constitution of Committee of Review | Minister must appoint a Committee: a ≥7-year lawyer (Chair), an Association/dental-association nominee, a consumer-interest appointee (never a practitioner), and one other suitably experienced appointee. |
| 109 | Date and place of hearing | Chairperson fixes date/place; 14 days' notice to parties. |
| 110 | Right of appearance | Parties appear in person or by agent; legal representation only with all parties' consent and Committee leave; hearings may be wholly/partly private. |
| 111 | Powers and procedure before a Committee | Royal Commissions Act powers/protections apply; Chairperson decides law/procedure questions; majority decides otherwise; Chairperson has a casting vote on a 2-2 split. |
| 112 | Determination of appeal | Committee determines and may order; may refer conduct concerns to the Medical/Dental Council as a complaint; re-appointment orders limited where positions are already filled by better applicants (unless the order names whose appointment yields). |
| 113 | Effect and implementation of order | Order has effect from the date made and is the org's final decision; a re-appointment order that displaces another practitioner terminates that practitioner's appointment with no compensation. |

## Key obligations and powers

- **Obligation** (s.105) — org must give written notice + reasons within 14 days.
- **Power** (s.106) — person may appeal to the Minister, subject to carve-outs.
- **Obligation** (s.108(1)) — Minister **must** appoint a Committee of Review on receipt of a
  valid appeal notice.
- **Obligation** (s.109) — Chairperson must give 14 days' hearing notice.
- **Power** (s.112(1A)) — Committee may refer misconduct/impairment concerns to a professional
  council.
