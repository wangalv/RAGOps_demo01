The centralised-employment structure that underlies the whole Act: almost everyone working in the
public health system is legally employed in the [[nsw-health-service]] (Crown employment, not
Public Service), not by the individual [[public-health-organisation]] they work for.

## Sections

| s. | Heading | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| 115 | The NSW Health Service | Persons employed under this Part by the Government in the service of the Crown, not in the Public Service. |
| 116 | Employment of staff generally | Government may employ staff to enable LHDs/statutory health corporations/hospitals, declared AHOs, Health Secretary's ambulance and Pt 1A functions, Health Administration Corporation, Cancer Institute (NSW); [[health-secretary]] exercises employer functions generally, with carve-outs for LHD/specialty-network CE and senior-executive employer functions held by boards/CEs. |
| 116A | Salary, conditions (non-senior-executive) | Health Secretary fixes salary/wages/conditions; may direct orgs to pay staff costs on the Government's behalf; may agree conditions with representative associations, binding on the whole group/class. |
| 116B | Special provisions for declared AHO staff | Employment conditional on the AHO's satisfaction re suitability given its health care philosophy; Anti-Discrimination Act s.56 applies. |
| 116C | Transfer on ground of redundancy | Health Secretary may direct transfer on redundancy grounds (excess numbers or unsuitable skill mix), subject to qualification/suitability tests; refusal without valid reason may lead to dismissal with no compensation. |
| 116D | Use of services/facilities outside NSW Health Service | Health Secretary may arrange secondment/use of other government sector agency staff/facilities. |
| 116E | Declared AHO obligations under other legislation | Declared AHO treated as the WHS "person conducting the business" and Anti-Discrimination Act "employer" for its assigned staff. |
| 116F | Privacy legislation | Staff enabling a public health org's functions are treated as part of that org for privacy law purposes. |
| 116G | Civil liability provisions | Workers Compensation Act Pt 5 applies as if the org were an additional employer; Government subrogated to org's insurance rights; contract-of-employment continuity rules on role changes. |
| 116H | Role of Health Secretary in industrial proceedings | Health Secretary is treated as the employer in industrial tribunal proceedings concerning NSW Health Service members. |

## Key obligations and powers

- **Power** (s.116(3)) — Health Secretary exercises employer functions generally, subject to
  carve-outs (s.116(3A)–(3D)) reserving CE/senior-executive employer functions to LHD/specialty
  network boards and CEs.
- **Power** (s.116C(1)) — Health Secretary may direct redundancy-based transfer, subject to
  qualification tests.
- **Consequence** (s.116C(2)) — unjustified refusal of transfer may lead to dismissal, no
  compensation.
- **Restriction** (s.116B(1)) — declared AHO staffing conditional on the AHO's suitability
  assessment (health care philosophy).
