The internal review pathway for a person disputing an ambulance fee, including protections while
a review is pending.

## Sections

| s. | Heading | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| 67Y | Application for review | Person charged may apply to [[health-secretary]] for review, per payment rules, with required supporting info. |
| 67Z | Review by Health Secretary | Health Secretary must conduct a review if duly applied for, subject to exceptions (already reviewed, application >7 days after debt-notice due date, missing required info, prescribed circumstances); may also review of own motion. Declared a statutory internal review under the State Debt Recovery Act 2018. |
| 67ZA | Extension of time — on-time applications | While an on-time review application is pending: no debt notice/Chief Commissioner referral, and payment requirement suspended; afterward the payment time is extended by the pending period. |
| 67ZB | Request for additional information | Health Secretary may request additional info; applicant must respond within 14 days (or a longer specified period), else review proceeds without it. |
| 67ZC | Actions after review | Health Secretary may revoke the charge, waive it, or confirm (with/without changed arrangements); **must** revoke if the person is exempt, there was a mistake of identity, or the amount was wrong. |
| 67ZD | Notice of outcome | Health Secretary must notify the applicant within 42 days of receipt (extended by any additional-information request period). |

## Key obligations and powers

- **Obligation** (s.67Z(1)) — must conduct a review if duly applied for (subject to listed
  exceptions).
- **Obligation** (s.67ZC(2)) — must revoke the fee decision in three specified circumstances.
- **Obligation** (s.67ZD(1)) — must notify the outcome within 42 days.
- **Protection** (s.67ZA(1)) — no debt notice/referral, and payment suspended, while an on-time
  review is pending.
