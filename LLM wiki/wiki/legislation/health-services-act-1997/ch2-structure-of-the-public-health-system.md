Defines the public health system's building blocks: what counts as a public health organisation,
and the three kinds of it ([[local-health-district]], [[statutory-health-corporation]],
[[affiliated-health-organisation]]), plus their primary purposes and functions.

## Sections

| s. | Heading | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| 6 | What is the public health system? | Comprises all LHDs, all statutory health corporations, all affiliated health organisations (re their recognised establishments/services), and the Health Secretary re ambulance services (Ch.5A) and Pt 1A of Ch.10 services. |
| 7 | What is a public health organisation? | A LHD, a statutory health corporation, or an affiliated health organisation (re recognised establishments/services). |
| 8 | What is a local health district? | Constituted under s.17, listed in Schedule 1. |
| 9 | Primary purposes of local health districts | Relief of sick/injured; promote/protect/maintain community health. |
| 10 | Functions of local health districts | Extensive list: conduct hospitals, ensure patient care standards, consult HCCC and health professionals, plan services, administer AHO funding delegated under s.129, etc. |
| 11 | What is a statutory health corporation? | Constituted under s.41, listed in Schedule 2. |
| 12 | Functions of statutory health corporations | Conduct hospitals/services as directed by the Minister under s.53; maintain standards; efficient operation. |
| 13 | What is an affiliated health organisation? | An org/institution recognised under s.62, only re its recognised establishments/services (Schedule 3). |
| 14 | Functions of affiliated health organisations | Maintain standards and efficient operation of recognised establishments/services. |
| 15 | What is a public hospital? | Controlled by a LHD, a statutory health corporation, an AHO (as recognised establishment), or the Crown. |
| 16 | (Repealed) | — |

## Key obligations and powers

No "must"/"may" operative provisions here — these are defining sections. Functions lists in s.10,
s.12, s.14 describe scope of activity rather than imposing enforceable duties per se.
