Empowers the [[health-secretary]] to directly provide shared/support/coordinating services across
the public health system (a shared-services function distinct from any individual org's own
Chapter 3/4/5 powers), and gives the Minister power to mandate acquisition of those services.

## Sections

| s. | Heading | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| 126A | (Repealed) | — |
| 126B | Health Secretary to provide certain services | May provide services to support the system/orgs, or to coordinate multi-org/State-wide provision; may contract with non-public-health-org persons (Minister approval needed); may set fees/charges; delegable to appointed bodies (with sub-delegation). |
| 126C | Appointed bodies | Health Secretary may appoint a committee/board for this Part; 5-year terms, fees/allowances, good-faith immunity (liability falls to Health Secretary). |
| 126D–126F | (Repealed) | — |
| 126G | Directions on acquisition of services | Minister may order a public health org to acquire specified services from the Health Secretary/another specified person; conduct under this section is specifically authorised for Competition and Consumer Act / Competition Code purposes, limited to what would otherwise contravene those Acts. |
| 126H | Consent of AHOs required for certain orders | Minister cannot direct an AHO under s.126G without its written consent. |

## Key obligations and powers

- **Power** (s.126B) — Health Secretary may provide shared/coordinating services and contract for
  them.
- **Power** (s.126G) — Minister may mandate service acquisition from a specified provider.
- **Restriction** (s.126H) — AHO consent required for any s.126G direction affecting it — echoes
  the consent theme running through [[ch5-affiliated-health-organisations]].
- **Competition law carve-out** (s.126G(2)–(3)) — specific statutory authorisation for conduct
  under this Part, bounded to what would otherwise breach competition law.
