Fee-charging and collection rules for general health services (as distinct from Chapter 5A's
ambulance-specific regime), including a protection for patients without means and a charge on
damages recovered by injured patients.

## Sections

| s. | Heading | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| 69 | Scale of fees | Minister fixes/amends/revokes by Gazette order; may adopt a Commonwealth body's scale by reference. |
| 70 | Liability of persons for health service fees | Recipients of chargeable services liable to contribute per the scale; recoverable as a debt; org may remit/postpone/write-off. |
| 71 | Care and treatment to be provided regardless of means | A person without means must not be refused care/treatment for sickness/injury at a public hospital. |
| 72 | Cost of relief a charge on damages | If an injured debtor has a damages claim against another, the org's fee liability is a charge on the damages, enforceable by the org's own action. |
| 73 | Order not to be made in certain circumstances | Court must not make an order under this Chapter if it would be unreasonable given the person's means. |
| 74 | Fees for medical attendance | Practitioner cannot charge a non-chargeable-hospital-service patient; contravening contracts unenforceable. |
| 75 | Hospital/other health services provided by Crown | Extends "public health organisation" references in this Chapter to Crown-controlled hospitals. |

## Key obligations and powers

- **Prohibition** (s.71) — a person without means must not be refused care/treatment.
- **Power** (s.69) — Minister fixes the scale of fees.
- **Power** (s.72) — organisation may bring an action to enforce its charge on damages.
- **Restriction** (s.73) — court must not make an unreasonable order.
- **Prohibition** (s.74(1)) — practitioner cannot charge for non-chargeable hospital service
  treatment.

## Cross-references

- s.75 broadens scope to Crown-controlled hospitals — relevant wherever "public health
  organisation" appears elsewhere for charging purposes.
