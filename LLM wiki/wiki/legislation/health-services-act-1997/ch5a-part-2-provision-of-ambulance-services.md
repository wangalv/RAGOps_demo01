Establishes the [[ambulance-service-of-nsw]], the Health Secretary's ambulance functions, the
Ambulance Service Advisory Board, and offences protecting the ambulance monopoly.

## Sections

| s. | Heading | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| 67AB | Appointed body | [[health-secretary]] may appoint a committee/board as an "appointed body" for the Chapter; 5-year terms, fees/allowances, no personal liability for good-faith acts (liability falls to Health Secretary instead). |
| 67A | Ambulance Service of NSW | Comprises NSW Health Service staff employed primarily for ambulance services; may have its own CE (a senior executive). |
| 67B | Provision etc of ambulance services | Health Secretary's functions on behalf of the Crown: provide/operate ambulance services, protect persons from injury, plan, set standards, inform the public; may delegate to an appointed body. |
| 67C | Ambulance Service Advisory Board | Established by the Act; CE + 8–12 Health Secretary-appointed members with relevant expertise; advises the Health Secretary/appointed bodies. |
| 67D | (Repealed) | — |
| 67E | Unauthorised provision of ambulance transport | Offence — providing fee/reward transport for sick/injured or similar operations without Health Secretary consent. **Max penalty 50 penalty units.** Exemptions for St John Ambulance, Royal Flying Doctor Service, mines rescue bodies. |
| 67F | Unauthorised collections | Offence — collecting money/property for ambulance services without consent. **Max penalty 50 penalty units.** |
| 67G | False calls | Offence — knowingly requesting an unneeded ambulance. **Max penalty 50 penalty units.** |
| 67H | Honorary ambulance officers | Health Secretary may appoint; unremunerated; subject to Health Secretary control. |
| 67I | Exculpation from personal liability | Staff/honorary officers not liable for good-faith injury/damage in carrying out ambulance duties. |
| 67J | (Repealed) | — |

## Key obligations and powers

- **Offences** (s.67E, s.67F, s.67G) — three distinct 50-penalty-unit offences protecting the
  ambulance service monopoly and preventing false calls.
- **Power** (s.67B(3)) — Health Secretary may delegate ambulance functions to an appointed body.
- **Power** (s.67E(2)) — Health Secretary may revoke consent/vary conditions given under s.67E.
