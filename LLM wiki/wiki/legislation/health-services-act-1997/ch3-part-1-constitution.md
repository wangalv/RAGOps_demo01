Constitutes local health districts, defines their geographic areas, and sets out how the Governor
can amend, dissolve, amalgamate, or rename them.

## Sections

| s. | Heading | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| 17 | Constitution of local health districts | LHDs constituted as listed in Schedule 1, each a body corporate. |
| 18 | Areas in respect of which LHDs constituted | Area defined by reference to local government areas (Sch 1 col 2) or other description (col 3). |
| 19 | Amendment of Schedule 1 | Governor may amend LHD names/areas by order published on the NSW legislation website. |
| 20 | Dissolution, amalgamation or change of name | Governor may dissolve/amalgamate/rename a LHD by order; must specify a public-interest opinion from the [[minister]]. |
| 21 | Consequential and transitional provisions | Schedule 4 applies to orders under this Part. |
| 22 | Corporate nature provisions | LHD has perpetual succession, an official seal, can sue/be sued, does not represent the Crown, and **cannot employ staff** (staff are employed via [[nsw-health-service]], Ch.9 Pt 1). |

## Key obligations and powers

- **Power** (s.19, s.20) — the Governor may, by order, amend, dissolve, amalgamate, or rename
  LHDs.
- **Obligation-adjacent condition** (s.20(3)) — an order under s.20 is not to be made unless the
  Minister is of the opinion it's in the public interest and has recommended it to the Governor.
- **Restriction** (s.22(2)) — a LHD cannot employ any staff.
