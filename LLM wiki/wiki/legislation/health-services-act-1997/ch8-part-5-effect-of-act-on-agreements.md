A single savings section: this Act doesn't override existing agreements with
[[visiting-practitioner]]s except through Parts 2–4 of this Chapter.

## Sections

| s. | Heading | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| 114 | Application of Act to agreements | Applies to any performance-of-work agreement between a public health organisation and a visiting practitioner; a provision of the Act/regulations/by-laws inconsistent with such an agreement has no effect on the practitioner **except** as provided by Parts 2, 3, and 4 of this Chapter. |

## Key obligations and powers

None operative — a scope/savings provision.
