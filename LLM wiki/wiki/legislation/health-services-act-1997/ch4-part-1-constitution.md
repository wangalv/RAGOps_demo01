Mirrors [[ch3-part-1-constitution]] for statutory health corporations: constitution, amendment of
the governing Schedule, dissolution/transfer/amalgamation, and corporate-nature provisions.

## Sections

| s. | Heading | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| 41 | Constitution of statutory health corporations | Constituted as listed in Schedule 2; each is chief-executive-governed, board-governed, or specialty-network-governed. |
| 42 | Amendment of Schedule 2 | Governor may amend Schedule 2 (names, or governance type changes) by order. |
| 43 | Dissolution, transfer, amalgamation, governance change | Governor may dissolve/transfer to a LHD/amalgamate/change governance by order; requires Minister's public-interest opinion. |
| 44 | Consequential and transitional provisions | Schedule 4 applies to orders under this Part. |
| 45 | Corporate nature provisions | Perpetual succession, official seal, sue/be sued, doesn't represent the Crown, **cannot employ staff**. |

## Key obligations and powers

- **Power** (s.42, s.43) — Governor may amend/dissolve/transfer/amalgamate/change governance by
  order.
- **Precondition** (s.43(3)) — order requires the Minister's opinion that it's in the public
  interest.
- **Restriction** (s.45(2)) — cannot employ staff directly.
