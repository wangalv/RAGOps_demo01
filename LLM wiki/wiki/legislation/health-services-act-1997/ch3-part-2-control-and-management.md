Establishes the two organs that run a local health district: the chief executive (Division 1) and
the local health district board (Division 2), plus power for the chief executive to create
sub-committees (Division 3).

## Key obligations and powers

- **Obligation** (s.28(2)) — a LHD board must not exercise a function inconsistently with a
  Health Secretary function.
- **Power** (s.29(1)) — the Minister may remove board members "for any reason or no reason and
  without notice."
- **Obligation** (s.29(2)) — Minister must make a Parliamentary statement on appointing an
  administrator, as soon as reasonably practicable.
- **Power** (s.29B) — chief executive may establish committees/councils.
