The core oversight powers of the [[health-secretary]]: general functions, inquiry powers,
authorised-officer inspection powers, and performance agreements. Also applies to the Cancer
Institute (NSW) as if it were a statutory health corporation.

## Sections

| s. | Heading | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| 122 | Functions of the Health Secretary | Broad function list: standards, efficiency, inquiries, governance/oversight/control of statutory health organisations, inspections, funding recommendations, performance agreements, directions (including re senior executive employment). |
| 123 | Inquiries by Health Secretary | May inquire into non-public-health-org bodies receiving Consolidated Fund money for health services, **except** private health facilities/nursing homes; delegable. |
| 124 | Authorised officers | Health Secretary appoints, issues certificates of authority (with specified content); officer must hold a certificate to act; extra preconditions (notice, reasonable hour, minimal force, residential consent) for non-public-health-org premises. |
| 125 | Powers of entry and inspection | Authorised officer may enter public health org/relevant premises; inspect, examine, copy/remove records, require assistance/answers/document production; Minister must pay compensation for damage to non-public-health-org premises unless obstruction occurred. |
| 126 | Performance agreements | Health Secretary may enter agreements with public health orgs (incl. National Health Reform Agreement service-agreement content, targets, evaluation, reporting); org must exercise functions per the agreement as far as practicable; must report results within 3 months of year end; Health Secretary evaluates and reports to Minister. |
| 126AA | Provision or disclosure of information | Privilege against compelled production/disclosure for relevant-inquiry documents/communications; not admissible in proceedings without Health Secretary consent; Health Secretary may still release a final report at discretion. |

## Key obligations and powers

- **Power** (s.123, s.124, s.125) — Health Secretary's inquiry/inspection/entry powers, with
  express carve-outs (private health facilities, nursing homes) and procedural preconditions for
  non-public-health-org premises.
- **Obligation** (s.126(4)) — org must report performance-agreement results within 3 months of
  financial year end.
- **Obligation** (s.125(3)) — Minister must pay reasonable compensation for inspection-caused
  damage to non-public-health-org premises (unless obstruction occurred).
- **Protection** (s.126AA) — inquiry-related documents/communications are privileged from
  compelled disclosure.
