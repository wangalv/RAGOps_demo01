Employment framework for [[nsw-health-service-senior-executive]]s — substantially rewritten in
2016 (Sch 2 [20]), which repealed the original Divisions and replaced them with a flatter
structure (s.121A–121K). ss.121L–121T are spent/repealed.

## Sections

| s. | Heading | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| 121A | NSW Health Service senior executives | Staff employed in a Public Service senior executive band; CEs of LHDs, specialty network corporations, and Ambulance Service of NSW **must** be senior executives; other statutory health corporation CEs may be; certain GSE Act Pt 4 role-holders may be designated. |
| 121B | Employer of senior executives | Defines "employer": Health Secretary generally, except LHD/specialty-network CE (employer = board) and other senior executives of those orgs (employer = CE). |
| 121C | Kinds of employment | Ongoing or term employment. |
| 121D | Employment in bands and assignment to roles | Employer sets band per work-level standards; LHD/specialty-network numbers/bands/roles need Health Secretary approval; assignment/transfer rules across NSW Health Service, Public Service, Police, Transport Service, with consultation/concurrence requirements especially for CE roles. |
| 121E | Government sector employment rules | Public Service Commissioner may make rules on senior executive employment, after consulting Health Secretary; inconsistent Health Secretary directions need Commissioner agreement (or consultation, for health/safety-related ones). |
| 121F | Contract of employment | Written contract; rules may prescribe model/mandatory terms; must address band, duration, remuneration package, performance, progression, leave, termination compensation. |
| 121G | Remuneration, benefits and allowances | Package within the Statutory and Other Offices Remuneration Act range for the band, unless Health Secretary sets a higher range per agreed parameters; benefits/allowances determined by Health Secretary. |
| 121H | Termination of employment | Employer (or Health Secretary if not employer) may terminate at any time, any/no reason, without notice; compensation only per contract; re-employment restrictions during compensation period; employer needs Health Secretary concurrence to terminate. |
| 121I | Acting in executive roles | Assignment to act when the substantive holder is unavailable/absent; full functions while acting but doesn't confer permanent executive status. |
| 121J | Industrial or legal proceedings excluded | Senior executive employment is not an "industrial matter" under the Industrial Relations Act; IRC unfair-dismissal Parts don't apply; no prerogative-writ relief. |
| 121K | Delegation of employer functions | Board employer functions delegable to chairperson/member (not employ/terminate); CE employer functions delegable to NSW Health Service members or Public Service staff. |

## Key obligations and powers

- **Mandatory senior-executive status** (s.121A(2)) — LHD CEs, specialty network CEs, Ambulance
  Service of NSW CE must be senior executives.
- **Power** (s.121H(1)) — employer may terminate at any time, without notice, for any/no reason.
- **Restriction** (s.121H(5)) — employer needs Health Secretary concurrence to terminate.
- **Exclusion** (s.121J) — senior executive employment disputes are removed from ordinary
  industrial jurisdiction entirely.
