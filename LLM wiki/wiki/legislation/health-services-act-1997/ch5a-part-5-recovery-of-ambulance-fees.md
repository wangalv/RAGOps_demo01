Connects unpaid ambulance debt to the State's general debt-recovery machinery under the *State
Debt Recovery Act 2018*, via the Chief Commissioner.

## Sections

| s. | Heading | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| 67U | Ambulance fee is debt payable | A debt-noticed fee is a debt to the [[health-secretary]], recoverable in court — unless/until referred to the Chief Commissioner for a debt recovery order. |
| 67V | (Repealed) | — |
| 67W | Information to Chief Commissioner | Health Secretary must provide specified identifying/service information to enable Chief Commissioner debt recovery action. |
| 67X | Suspension of debt recovery action | Health Secretary may request suspension, or revocation of suspension, of debt recovery action by notice to the Chief Commissioner. |

## Key obligations and powers

- **Obligation** (s.67W) — Health Secretary must provide the specified information to the Chief
  Commissioner for referred debts.
- **Power** (s.67U(2)) — Health Secretary may recover the debt via court proceedings directly
  (until referral).
- **Power** (s.67X) — Health Secretary may request suspension/revocation of debt recovery action.
