Three governance models, one Division each: board governed (Div 1), chief executive governed
(Div 2), and specialty network governed (Div 3, which imports Ch.3 Pt 2 Div 2's board provisions
by modification).

## Sections

| s. | Heading | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| 46 | Constitution of health corporation boards | A board constituted for each board governed health corporation, named "[Corporation] Board". |
| 47 | Board to control affairs | Board controls affairs of the corporation. |
| 48 | Board subject to Minister | Board is subject to Minister's control/direction, except re content of a report/recommendation to the Minister. |
| 49 | Membership of health corporation board | CE (ex officio) + 5–11 Minister-appointed members; ≥1 must be a NSW Health Service member connected to the corporation (unless <50 staff). |
| 50 | Members/procedure provisions | Schedule 5 applies. |
| 51 | Appointment of CE (board governed) | Appointed by [[health-secretary]]; manages affairs; subject to board's control/direction. |
| 52 | Removal of members and administrator | Governor may remove members/CE at any time, or appoint an administrator. |
| 52A | Appointment of CE (chief executive governed) | Appointed by Health Secretary; employed in NSW Health Service. |
| 52B | CE to manage and control affairs | As for board-governed corporations but the CE runs it directly. |
| 52C | Functions of CE generally | Has functions conferred by this/other Acts. |
| 52D | Advisory councils | Minister may establish an advisory council per chief-executive-governed corporation. |
| 52E | Other committees and councils | CE may establish committees/councils. |
| 52F | Boards of specialty network governed corporations | A board established for each; Ch.3 Pt 2 Div 2 (LHD board provisions) applied with modifications. |
| 52G | Appointment of CE (specialty network governed) | Appointed by the network's board with Health Secretary concurrence; a NSW Health Service senior executive. |

## Key obligations and powers

- **Power** (s.52) — Governor may remove board members/CE "for any reason or no reason" at any
  time.
- **Restriction** (s.48) — board subject to Minister's control/direction except re contents of
  reports/recommendations to the Minister.
- **Structural device** (s.52F(2)) — applies Ch.3 Pt 2 Div 2 (LHD board rules) to specialty
  network boards via textual substitution ("board" for "local health district board" etc.).
