The general Governor's-order power to move hospitals/services/property between statutory health
organisations (referenced by notes throughout Chapters 3 and 4).

## Sections

| s. | Heading | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| 131 | Transfer between statutory health organisations | Governor may transfer (by order) hospitals/institutions/services/support-services/assets/rights/liabilities between statutory health organisations, or from Crown control, and amend Schedules 1/2 accordingly; requires the Minister's public-interest opinion; no inquiry required. |
| 132 | Consequential and transitional provisions | Schedule 4 applies to orders under this Part. |

## Key obligations and powers

- **Power** (s.131(1)) — Governor may transfer by order, on the Minister's public-interest
  opinion (s.131(3)).

## Cross-references

- This is the general transfer mechanism the notes in [[ch3-part-1-constitution]] (s.20) and
  [[ch4-part-1-constitution]] (s.43) point to ("Part 3 of Chapter 10 provides for the transfer...").
- Distinct from s.64 ([[ch5-affiliated-health-organisations]]), which requires AHO consent for
  the equivalent transfer involving an affiliated health organisation.
