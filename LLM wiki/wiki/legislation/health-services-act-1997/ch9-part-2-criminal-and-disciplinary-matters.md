The staff-side mirror of [[ch8-part-3-criminal-and-disciplinary-matters]] (visiting practitioners):
mandatory disclosure, mandatory reporting to professional councils, and disciplinary powers for
serious sex/violence offences, plus a broader suspension power for registration/prohibition
issues.

## Sections

| s. | Heading | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| 117AA | Definitions | "Member of staff" = NSW Health Service member employed under Pt 1 to enable a public health org's functions. |
| 117 | Duty to report criminal conduct/disciplinary matters | Staff member must report, within 7 days, a charge/conviction for a [[serious-sex-or-violence-offence]] or a misconduct finding, to the chief executive of the relevant org. |
| 117A | Duty of chief executive to report conduct | Chief executive must report suspected professional misconduct to the relevant Council; Ambulance Service of NSW treated as a public health organisation for this purpose; dual-reporting satisfied by compliance with either regime. |
| 118 | Disciplinary action for serious sex/violence offences | Chief executive must notify [[health-secretary]] within 30 days; staff member gets a submission opportunity; Health Secretary may take disciplinary action having regard to s.119; special rules for senior-executive transfers to CE roles (board concurrence needed). |
| 119 | Protection of patients/clients/children paramount | Paramount consideration for disciplinary decisions under this Part. |
| 120 | No compensation for termination | No compensation payable for termination under this Part. |
| 120A | Suspension pending misconduct/serious criminal charge decision | Health Secretary may suspend a staff member whose registration is suspended/conditioned, who is subject to an interim prohibition order, or who is charged with a **serious criminal offence** (≥5 years imprisonment); salary may be withheld and, on adverse outcome, forfeited to the State unless the Health Secretary directs otherwise. |
| 121 | Effect of Part | Doesn't affect Industrial Relations Act unfair-dismissal rights (IRC must have regard to s.119) or Criminal Records Act spent-convictions protection. |

## Key obligations and powers

- **Obligation** (s.117) — staff must report a charge/conviction/finding within 7 days.
- **Obligation** (s.117A(1)) — chief executive must report suspected misconduct to the relevant
  Council.
- **Obligation** (s.118(1)) — chief executive must notify Health Secretary within 30 days.
- **Power** (s.118(3)) — Health Secretary may take disciplinary action having regard to s.119.
- **Power** (s.120A(1)) — Health Secretary may suspend on registration suspension/interim
  prohibition/serious criminal charge grounds; salary withholding/forfeiture powers attached.
