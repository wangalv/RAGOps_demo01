Defines the [[visiting-practitioner]] category and its subtypes — the contract-based (not
employment-based) regime for doctors/dentists working at public hospitals.

## Sections

| s. | Heading | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| 76 | Who is a visiting practitioner? | A medical practitioner/dentist appointed (not as employee) by a public health organisation to practise at its facilities per the appointment's conditions. |
| 77 | Kinds of visiting practitioners | Those under a service contract (visiting medical officer or honorary medical officer) and those appointed otherwise. |
| 78 | Who is a visiting medical officer? | Appointed under a service contract, for monetary remuneration. |
| 79 | Who is an honorary medical officer? | Appointed under an honorary contract, i.e. not for monetary remuneration. |
