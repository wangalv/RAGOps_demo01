Closing provisions: Samaritan Funds, liability protections, information sharing, a
public-private-partnership prohibition, offences, evidentiary shortcuts, the regulation-making
power, and a statutory review clause.

## Sections

| s. | Heading | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| 133 | Establishment of Samaritan Funds | Unclaimed patient money/effects (12 months after death or discharge) become org property, held in a Samaritan Fund for needy patients; regulations govern management; separate or single fund per LHD/statutory health corporation; extends to Crown-controlled hospitals. |
| 133A | Nature of staffing arrangements | Crown-org staffing arrangements aren't an "employment placement service" under the Fair Trading Act. |
| 133B | Personal liability of board members | Good-faith acts/omissions by a statutory health organisation/board/member/agent don't attract personal liability. |
| 133C | Sharing appointment information | Public health org may share health-practitioner appointment info with a private health facility licensee where reasonable safety concerns exist; good-faith disclosure protected from liability. |
| 133D | Consolidation of annual reporting | NSW Health entities' annual reporting info may be consolidated into the Ministry of Health's under the GSF Act "lead agency" mechanism. |
| 133E | Prohibition of certain PPP arrangements | State/LHD/statutory health corporation must not enter arrangements for private construction + provision of acute hospital services to public patients, where the entity retains an interest and the building is privately financed/owned/controlled; regulation-excludable; doesn't affect existing arrangements. |
| 134 | Obstructing/hindering officials | Offence — obstructing Minister/Health Secretary/authorised officer/other public official in exercising Act functions. **Max penalty 100 penalty units.** |
| 135 | Authentication of documents | Public health org documents authenticated by CE or CE-authorised employee signature (no seal required). |
| 136 | Proof of certain matters not required | Evidentiary presumption (rebuttable) for board constitution, resolutions, appointments, quorum. |
| 137 | Proceedings for offences | Dealt with summarily before the Local Court (except s.67J(2), now repealed). |
| 138 | (Repealed) | — |
| 139 | Liability of persons conducting performance reviews | Good-faith expert reviewers of practitioner/employee performance or system operation aren't personally liable; liability falls to the org/Health Secretary instead. |
| 139A | Liability assisting under Guardianship/Care and Protection Acts | NSW Health Service staff assisting a registered practitioner under specified guardianship/child-protection provisions aren't personally liable; liability falls to the LHD/statutory health corporation; doesn't relieve the practitioner of treatment-consent liability. |
| 140 | Regulations | Governor may make regulations; extensive illustrative list (accounts, records, information, visiting practitioners, admission/discharge/fees, fundraising, AHO administration, unclaimed property, staff discipline, employment conditions for non-declared AHO staff, funding accounting, long service leave recognition, out-of-date reference updates, smoke-free areas); regulations may create offences up to 5 penalty units; visiting-practitioner regulations need Medical Services Committee advice. |
| 141 | Savings and transitional provisions | Schedule 7 applies. |
| 142 | Review of Act | Minister must review the Act's policy objectives/appropriateness starting ~5 years after assent; report tabled in Parliament within 12 months of that 5-year mark. |

## Key obligations and powers

- **Offence** (s.134) — obstructing/hindering officials, 100 penalty units (the Act's highest
  penalty).
- **Prohibition** (s.133E(2)) — State/LHD/statutory health corporation must not enter specified
  PPP arrangements for acute hospital construction/service provision.
- **Power** (s.140(1)) — broad Governor regulation-making power, with an illustrative (not
  exhaustive) list.
- **Obligation** (s.142) — Minister must review the Act ~5 years after assent and table a report
  within 12 months of that point.
