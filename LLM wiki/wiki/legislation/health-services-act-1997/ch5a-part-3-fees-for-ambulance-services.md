The substantive fee-charging rules: who can be charged, who is liable, exemptions, and the rules
governing fee levels and waivers.

## Sections

| s. | Heading | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| 67K | Health Secretary may charge fee | May charge for ambulance services provided; fee capped at the scale of fees in force. |
| 67KA | Booking/cancellation fees | Health Secretary may charge private providers (private health facility licensees, residential care home operators, prescribed providers) for bookings and late cancellations. |
| 67L | Scale of fees | Minister fixes/amends/revokes the scale of fees by order in the Gazette. |
| 67M | Who is liable for payment | The person receiving services, except children — parents/guardians are jointly/severally liable instead, with a right of contribution between them, enforceable/reducible by a court. |
| 67N | Exemptions | Exempt if fee not payable under Health Insurance Levies Act 1982 s.17, holder of a prescribed concession card, or otherwise exempt under regulations/payment rules. |
| 67O | Payment rules | Health Secretary may make payment rules by Gazette order (exemptions, waiver/reduction, time extensions, instalments, fee reviews); must not be inconsistent with the Act/regulations. |

## Key obligations and powers

- **Power** (s.67K, s.67KA) — Health Secretary may charge ambulance fees and booking/cancellation
  fees.
- **Obligation** (s.67M(3)) — each parent/guardian of a child receiving services is jointly and
  severally liable.
- **Power** (s.67L) — Minister fixes the scale of fees.
- **Power** (s.67O) — Health Secretary makes payment rules, bounded by the Act/regulations.
