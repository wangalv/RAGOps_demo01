The funding mechanics for public health organisations: Consolidated Fund subsidies, loans,
AHO repayment agreements, and delegated AHO funding via local health districts.

## Sections

| s. | Heading | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| 126I | National Health Reform Agreement funding | Schedule 6A governs — not separately paginated (see [[health-services-act-1997-overview]]). |
| 127 | Determination of subsidies | [[minister]] considers population/need, external-patient services, net receipts/expenditure, capital needs (LHDs); broader discretion for statutory health corporations/AHOs; must also have regard to the National Health Reform Agreement; may attach conditions; breach triggers Health Secretary recommendations. |
| 127A | Deferral of payment | Minister may defer subsidy payment to a subsequent financial year. |
| 127B | Loans to public health organisations | Minister may determine loans from Consolidated Fund money, on determined terms; must not cause expenditure beyond forward estimates; GSF Act Pt 6 disapplied. |
| 128 | Agreement by AHO to repay sums | Minister may require an AHO (or property-holding trustees) to agree to repay/pay sums before Consolidated Fund money is paid; no breach of trust for compliance. |
| 129 | Funding of AHO recognised establishments/services | Minister may delegate to a LHD the function of determining an AHO's subsidy and conditions. |
| 130 | Performance agreements (LHD–AHO) | A LHD exercising the s.129 delegation may enter a performance agreement with the AHO; sets targets, requires reporting within 3 months of year end to the LHD, which evaluates/reports to the Health Secretary. |

## Key obligations and powers

- **Power** (s.127, s.127A, s.127B) — Minister determines, defers, and lends subsidy money, with
  listed considerations and (for loans) a forward-estimates ceiling.
- **Power** (s.128) — Minister may require an AHO repayment agreement as a funding precondition.
- **Power** (s.129) — Minister may delegate AHO-funding determination to a LHD.
- **Obligation** (s.130(4)) — AHO must report performance results within 3 months of year end
  (mirrors s.126(4) for org-to-Health-Secretary reporting).

## Cross-references

- s.10(k) ([[ch2-structure-of-the-public-health-system]]) — LHD function of administering
