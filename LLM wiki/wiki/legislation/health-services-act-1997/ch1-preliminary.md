Machinery provisions: name, commencement, definitions signpost, objects, and a note on the legal
status of the Act's explanatory introductions.

## Sections

| s. | Heading | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Name of Act | This Act is the *Health Services Act 1997*. |
| 2 | Commencement | Commences on a day/days appointed by proclamation. |
| 3 | Definitions | Defined terms are in the [[health-services-act-1997-overview\|Dictionary]] at the end of the Act. |
| 4 | Objects of Act | Sets out the Act's 10 objects (see [[health-services-act-1997]]#Purpose). |
| 5 | Notes | Chapter introductions and other notes in the Act are explanatory only and don't form part of the Act. |

## Key obligations and powers

None — this Chapter is definitional/interpretive.

## Cross-references

- s.3 points to the Dictionary — see [[health-services-act-1997-overview]] for the definitions list.
- s.4 objects recur throughout as interpretive context for later Chapters, especially Ch.3–5
  ([[public-health-organisation]] structure) and Ch.8 ((g)–(h), visiting practitioner disclosure).
