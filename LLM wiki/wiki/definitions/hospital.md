**Statutory text** (Dictionary): Means an institution at which relief is given to sick or injured people through the provision of care or treatment.

**Plain language**: The base term underlying "public hospital" (s.15) and much of Chapters 3–5.

See [[ch2-structure-of-the-public-health-system]].
