**Statutory text** (Dictionary): Means a statutory health corporation that, as specified in Schedule 2, is specialty network governed.

**Plain language**: The third statutory health corporation governance model — governed via a board using LHD board rules by modification.

See [[ch4-part-2-control-and-management]], [[statutory-health-corporation]].
