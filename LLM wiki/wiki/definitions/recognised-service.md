**Statutory text** (Dictionary): Of an affiliated health organisation means a health service or health support service of the organisation that is listed in column 2 of Schedule 3 next to its name.

**Plain language**: The services-side counterpart to "recognised establishment."

See [[ch5-affiliated-health-organisations]], [[affiliated-health-organisation]].
