**Statutory text** (Dictionary): Means any person who is employed under Part 1 of Chapter 9 in the NSW Health Service.

**Plain language**: Anyone legally employed under the Act's central employment mechanism.

See [[ch9-part-1-employment-of-staff]], [[nsw-health-service]].
