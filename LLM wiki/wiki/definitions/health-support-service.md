**Statutory text** (Dictionary): Means a service that is provided in aid or support of a health service, such as — (a) the professional, technical or other education or training of persons to be employed within the public health system to provide health services, or (b) the procurement or supply (or both) of goods, substances or services to providers of health services, or (c) the planning or construction of any building or facility for use in the provision of health services, or (d) the provision of corporate services to providers of health services, and includes a service prescribed by the regulations.

**Plain language**: The back-office/enabling counterpart to "health service" — training, procurement, construction, corporate services.
