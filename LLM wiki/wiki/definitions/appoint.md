**Statutory text** (Dictionary): In relation to a visiting practitioner, includes re-appoint.

**Plain language**: Ensures re-appointment decisions are covered wherever "appoint" is used for visiting practitioners.

See [[ch8-part-1-classification-of-visiting-practitioners]].
