**Statutory text** (Dictionary): Has the same meaning as it has in Schedule 6A and includes any agreement that replaces or supersedes that Agreement.

**Plain language**: The Commonwealth-State funding agreement referenced throughout for performance agreements, health networks, and Schedule 6A funding arrangements.

See [[ch10-part-1-administration]], [[ch10-part-2-finance]].
