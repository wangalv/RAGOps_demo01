**Statutory text** (Dictionary): Means the Minister administering the Industrial Relations Act 1996 for the time being.

**Plain language**: The Minister who appoints the arbitrator for visiting-medical-officer contract disputes — distinct from the Health Minister.

See [[ch8-part-2-service-contracts]].
