**Statutory text** (Dictionary): (a) of a local health district — means the chief executive of that district, or (b) of a statutory health corporation — means the chief executive of that corporation, or (c) of an affiliated health organisation — means the person who is responsible to the governing body of the organisation for the management of its recognised establishments and recognised services.

**Plain language**: One term covering the equivalent role across all three kinds of public health organisation.

See [[ch3-part-2-control-and-management]], [[ch4-part-2-control-and-management]].
