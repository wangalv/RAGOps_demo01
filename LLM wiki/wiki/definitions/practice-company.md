**Statutory text** (Dictionary): Means a corporation (however incorporated) that is controlled or conducted by a medical practitioner and by means of which the medical practitioner conducts his or her medical practice.

**Plain language**: Lets a doctor's incorporated practice, rather than the doctor personally, be the contracting party to a service contract.

See [[ch8-part-2-service-contracts]].
