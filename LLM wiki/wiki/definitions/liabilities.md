**Statutory text** (Dictionary): Means any liabilities, debts or obligations (whether present or future, whether vested or contingent and whether personal or assignable).

**Plain language**: The counterpart to "assets" for transfer/liability provisions.

See [[ch10-part-3-transfer-of-hospitals]].
