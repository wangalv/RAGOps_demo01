**Statutory text** (Dictionary): Means any of the following — (a) any hospital service, (b) any medical service, (c) any paramedical service, (d) any community health service, (e) any environmental health service, (e1) the supply or fitting of any prosthesis or therapeutic device, (f) any other service (including any service of a class or description prescribed by the regulations) relating to the maintenance or improvement of the health, or the restoration to health, of persons or the prevention of disease in or injury to persons.

**Plain language**: A deliberately broad, extensible definition (regulation-expandable via (f)).
