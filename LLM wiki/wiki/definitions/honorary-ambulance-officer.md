**Statutory text** (s.67H): Means a person appointed to be an honorary ambulance officer under section 67H.

**Plain language**: An unremunerated Health-Secretary appointee who may carry out ambulance functions as directed.

See [[ch5a-part-2-provision-of-ambulance-services]].
