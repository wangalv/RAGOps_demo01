**Statutory text** (Dictionary): Means a conviction within the meaning of the Criminal Records Act 1991.

**Plain language**: Anchors the mandatory-disclosure regime to the external Criminal Records Act's definition, which also carries the spent-convictions protection referenced in ss.104, 121.

See [[ch8-part-3-criminal-and-disciplinary-matters]], [[ch9-part-2-criminal-and-disciplinary-matters]].
