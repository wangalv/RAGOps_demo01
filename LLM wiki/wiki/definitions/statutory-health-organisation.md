**Statutory text** (Dictionary): Means a local health district or a statutory health corporation.

**Plain language**: A narrower umbrella than "public health organisation" — excludes affiliated health organisations. Used in s.131 (transfer) and s.133B (liability).

See [[local-health-district]], [[statutory-health-corporation]], [[ch10-part-3-transfer-of-hospitals]], [[ch11-miscellaneous]].
