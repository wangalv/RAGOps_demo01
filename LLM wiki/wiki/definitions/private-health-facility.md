**Statutory text** (Dictionary): Means a private health facility within the meaning of the Private Health Facilities Act 2007.

**Plain language**: Cross-reference to another Act; relevant to the s.123 inquiry exemption and s.133C appointment-information sharing.

See [[ch10-part-1-administration]], [[ch11-miscellaneous]].
