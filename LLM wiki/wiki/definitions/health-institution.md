**Statutory text** (Dictionary): Means an institution (other than a hospital) by or at which health services or health support services are provided.

**Plain language**: The non-hospital counterpart to "hospital" — together they cover essentially all service delivery sites.

See [[ch2-structure-of-the-public-health-system]].
