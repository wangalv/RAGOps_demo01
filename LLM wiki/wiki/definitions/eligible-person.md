**Statutory text** (Dictionary): Has the same meaning as it has in the Health Insurance Act 1973 of the Commonwealth.

**Plain language**: The Medicare-eligible patient category the Medicare Principles and Commitments and general fee-charging provisions are built around.

See [[ch6-medicare-principles-and-commitments]], [[ch7-charges-for-health-services]].
