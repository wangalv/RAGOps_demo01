**Statutory text** (Dictionary): Means any health service provided to a patient by a public hospital that is a recognized hospital and for which it has been agreed under the Medicare Agreement that the patient is not to be charged, but only if the Medicare Agreement is in force at the time the service is provided.

**Plain language**: The Medicare-protected free-treatment category underlying s.71 (must-not-refuse-for-inability-to-pay) and s.74 (no doctor's fees).

See [[ch7-charges-for-health-services]].
