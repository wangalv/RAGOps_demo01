**Statutory text** (Dictionary): Means any rights, powers, privileges or immunities (whether present or future, whether vested or contingent and whether personal or assignable).

**Plain language**: The rights-side counterpart to "assets"/"liabilities" for transfer provisions.

See [[ch10-part-3-transfer-of-hospitals]].
