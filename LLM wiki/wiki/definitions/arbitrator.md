**Statutory text** (s.90): Means a person appointed under section 90 to be an arbitrator.

**Plain language**: A nominated Industrial Relations Commission judicial member, appointed by the relevant Minister to determine system-wide visiting-medical-officer contract terms.

See [[ch8-part-2-service-contracts]], [[industrial-relations-commission]].
