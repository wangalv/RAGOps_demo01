**Statutory text** (Dictionary): Means the Agreement (as in force from time to time) between the Commonwealth and the States referred to in section 24 of the Health Insurance Act 1973 of the Commonwealth.

**Plain language**: The intergovernmental agreement underlying "non-chargeable hospital service" and Medicare-eligible patient treatment.

See [[ch6-medicare-principles-and-commitments]], [[ch7-charges-for-health-services]].
