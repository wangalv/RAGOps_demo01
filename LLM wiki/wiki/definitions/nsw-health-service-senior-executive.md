**Statutory text** (s.121A): Means a person employed in the NSW Health Service as a NSW Health Service senior executive.

**Plain language**: Public Service senior-executive-band employees within the NSW Health Service — subject to a distinct employment regime under Chapter 9 Part 3.

See [[ch9-part-3-senior-executives]].
