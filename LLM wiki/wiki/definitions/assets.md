**Statutory text** (Dictionary): Means any legal or equitable estate or interest (whether present or future, whether vested or contingent and whether personal or assignable) in real or personal property of any description (including money), and includes securities, choses in action and documents.

**Plain language**: A broad, standard statutory definition of property for transfer/liability provisions.

See [[ch10-part-3-transfer-of-hospitals]].
