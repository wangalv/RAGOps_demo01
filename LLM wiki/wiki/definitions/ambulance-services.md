**Statutory text** (Dictionary (Ch.5A, s.3)): Means services relating to the work of rendering first aid to, and the transport of, sick and injured persons.

**Plain language**: The core activity Chapter 5A regulates.

See [[ch5a-part-2-provision-of-ambulance-services]].
