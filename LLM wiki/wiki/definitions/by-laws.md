**Statutory text** (Dictionary): Means by-laws under this Act.

**Plain language**: Subordinate rules a public health organisation can make (with ministerial/Health-Secretary approval) for management, patient services, visiting practitioners, and more.

See [[ch3-part-3-functions]] (s.39), [[ch4-part-3-functions]] (s.60), [[ch5-affiliated-health-organisations]] (s.63).
