Dictionary term "Association" — means the Australian Medical Association (NSW) Limited. The
statutory counterparty representing visiting medical officers in [[ch8-part-2-service-contracts]].

## Key powers

- Recommends condition sets for [[minister]] approval as standard service contracts (s.87).
- Applies (jointly or individually with the Minister) for appointment of an [[arbitrator]] to
  determine system-wide fee-for-service/sessional contract terms (s.89).
- Appears as of right before the arbitrator (s.93(1)).
- May appeal an arbitrator's determination to the Full Bench of the Industrial Relations
  Commission (s.96), or seek an interpretive declaration (s.97).
- Nominates a medical-practitioner member of a [[committee-of-review]] when the appellant is a
  medical practitioner (s.108(2)(b)).
