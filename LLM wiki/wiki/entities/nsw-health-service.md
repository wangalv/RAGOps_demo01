The centralised employment vehicle underlying the entire public health system: persons employed
under Chapter 9 Part 1 by the Government of NSW in the service of the Crown, but **not** in the
Public Service of New South Wales (s.115). Defined at s.115; Dictionary entry "NSW Health Service"
— see section 115.

## Why it exists

[[local-health-district]]s, [[statutory-health-corporation]]s, and declared
[[affiliated-health-organisation]]s all **cannot employ staff directly** (ss.22(2), 45(2),
62A(3)). Every person working for them — clinical and administrative — is legally an employee of
the NSW Health Service, assigned to enable that organisation's functions. This is why Chapter 9
Part 1 ([[ch9-part-1-employment-of-staff]]) is structurally load-bearing for the rest of the Act.

## Employer functions

Held by the [[health-secretary]] generally, with specific carve-outs: LHD/specialty-network board
holds employer functions for its own chief executive; that chief executive holds employer
functions for the organisation's other [[nsw-health-service-senior-executive|senior executives]]
(s.116(3)–(3D), s.121B).

## Also covers

- [[ambulance-service-of-nsw]] — staff employed primarily for ambulance services (s.67A(1)).
- Staff of the Health Administration Corporation and Cancer Institute (NSW) (s.116(1)(e)–(f)).
