Comprises [[nsw-health-service]] staff employed primarily in connection with the provision of
ambulance services under Chapter 5A (s.67A(1)). Dictionary: "see section 67A." May have its own
chief executive, appointed by the [[health-secretary]], who is a
[[nsw-health-service-senior-executive]] (s.67A(2)–(3), s.121A(2)(c)).

## Functions (held by the Health Secretary on its behalf, s.67B)

Provide/conduct/operate/maintain ambulance services; protect persons from injury/death; plan;
set standards; inform the public — delegable to an [[ambulance-service-advisory-board|appointed
body]].

## Fee regime

Runs its own fee-charging, invoicing, debt-recovery, and review machinery, distinct from the
general health-service fee regime in [[ch7-charges-for-health-services]] — see
[[ch5a-part-3-fees-for-ambulance-services]] through [[ch5a-part-7-miscellaneous]].

## Protections

Offences protect its operating monopoly: unauthorised transport-for-reward, unauthorised
collections, and false calls are all 50-penalty-unit offences (ss.67E–67G). Staff and honorary
officers have good-faith personal-liability exculpation (s.67I).
