Conceived the [[memex]] in 1945 — a personal, curated knowledge store with associative trails
between documents. Cited in [[llm-wiki-idea-doc]] as the intellectual forerunner of
[[llm-wiki-pattern]]: his vision was closer to a private, actively-curated wiki than to what the
web became, but he had no answer for who would do the ongoing maintenance. That's the gap this
vault's pattern claims to close, by having the LLM handle it.
