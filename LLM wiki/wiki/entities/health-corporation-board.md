Governs each board-governed [[statutory-health-corporation]] (s.46), named "[Corporation] Board".
Consists of the corporation's chief executive (ex officio) plus 5–11 [[minister]]-appointed
members, at least one a [[nsw-health-service]] member connected to the corporation (unless fewer
than 50 staff are employed there) (s.49). Procedure detail in Schedule 4A/5 (not separately
paginated).

## Key powers/status

Controls the corporation's affairs (s.47); subject to Minister control/direction **except** re
the content of its own reports/recommendations to the Minister (s.48). The
[[governor|Governor]] may remove members (including the chief executive) at any time, for any/no
reason, or appoint an administrator (s.52).
