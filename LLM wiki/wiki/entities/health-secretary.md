Defined in the Dictionary as the Secretary of the Ministry of Health. The single largest holder of
functions in [[health-services-act-1997]] — the day-to-day administrative head of the public
health system, distinct from the more political [[minister]].

## Key powers and functions

- General oversight functions: standards, efficiency, inquiries, governance/oversight/control of
  statutory health organisations, inspections, performance agreements, employment directions
  (s.122).
- Inquiry and inspection powers, including appointing [[authorised-officer|authorised officers]]
  with entry/seizure/questioning powers (ss.123–125).
- Exercises the Government's employer functions for the [[nsw-health-service]] generally, subject
  to carve-outs for LHD/specialty-network CE and senior-executive employer roles reserved to
  boards/CEs (s.116).
- Runs [[ambulance-service-of-nsw]] fee-charging, debt-recovery, and fee-review machinery
  (Chapter 5A) — the single busiest actor in that Chapter.
- Provides shared/coordinating services to the public health system (s.126B) and may make model
  by-laws for LHDs (s.39) and chief-executive/specialty-network-governed corporations (s.60).
- Must terminate or confirm retention of a visiting practitioner convicted of a serious sex/
  violence offence, on notification from an organisation's chief executive (s.100); parallel duty
  for NSW Health Service staff (s.118).
- Employer of NSW Health Service senior executives by default (s.121B), with termination power
  (s.121H) and industrial-jurisdiction exclusion (s.121J) for that role.
