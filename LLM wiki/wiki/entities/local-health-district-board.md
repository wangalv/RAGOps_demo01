Governs each [[local-health-district]] (one board per district). Constituted under s.26: 6–13
[[minister]]-appointed members with a required skills mix (health/business/financial management,
clinical service experience, university/research representation where appropriate, community
knowledge), at least one member with Aboriginal-health expertise and at least one
self-identifying Aboriginal member; terms ≤5 years, max 10 years total; one member is Chairperson.

## Key functions (s.28)

Approve clinical/corporate governance frameworks, systems, and strategic plans; provide strategic
oversight of financial/operational performance; appoint and exercise employer functions over the
chief executive; approve National Health Reform Agreement service agreements; consult community/
providers; endorse annual reporting information; liaise with other LHD/specialty-network boards.

## Key obligations

Must not exercise a function inconsistently with the [[health-secretary]] (s.28(2)).

## Removal

The [[minister]] may remove any or all members "for any reason or no reason and without notice"
(s.29(1)), and must then make a Parliamentary statement on the basis for any administrator
appointment (s.29(2)).

## Reused elsewhere

Applied by modification to specialty network governed [[statutory-health-corporation]] boards
(s.52F) — see [[ch4-part-2-control-and-management]].
