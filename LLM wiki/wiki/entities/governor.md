The formal order-making authority throughout [[health-services-act-1997]] — exercises structural
powers (constitution, dissolution, amalgamation, transfer, governance changes) on the recommendation
of the [[minister]], who must hold a public-interest opinion before recommending most such orders.

## Key powers

- Amend Schedule 1 (LHD names/areas), dissolve/amalgamate/rename LHDs (ss.19–20).
- Amend Schedule 2 (statutory health corporations), dissolve/transfer/amalgamate/change governance
  (ss.42–43).
- Amend Schedule 3 (affiliated health organisations) — but only with the organisation's consent
  (s.62(2), (4)).
- Transfer establishments/services of an AHO to a LHD/statutory health corporation, with AHO
  consent (s.64).
- Transfer hospitals/institutions/services/property between statutory health organisations
  (s.131).
- Remove health corporation board members or appoint an administrator (s.52(1)).
- Make regulations under the Act (s.140(1)).
