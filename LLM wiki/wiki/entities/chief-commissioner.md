External role defined by reference to the *State Debt Recovery Act 2018* (not created by this
Act). Dictionary (Chapter 5A-scoped, s.67AA): "has the same meaning as in the State Debt Recovery
Act 2018." Receives referred, unpaid ambulance debt for state-wide debt recovery action.

## Role in this Act

- [[health-secretary]] refers unpaid ambulance-fee debt notices to the Chief Commissioner for a
  debt recovery order (s.67U(3)).
- Health Secretary must provide specified identifying/service information for referred debts
  (s.67W).
- Health Secretary may request suspension or revocation of suspension of debt recovery action
  (s.67X).
- Health Secretary cannot change payment arrangements after referral unless the referral is
  revoked (s.67T(5)).
- Health Secretary cannot write off a referred debt unless the referral is revoked or action is
  suspended/cancelled (s.67ZE(2)).
