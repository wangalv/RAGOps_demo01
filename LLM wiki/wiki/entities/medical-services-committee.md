Established under s.20B of the *Health Administration Act 1982* (not this Act). Dictionary:
"means the Medical Services Committee established under section 20B of the Health Administration
Act 1982."

## Role in this Act

A recurring precondition-gate: model by-laws or by-laws governing visiting-practitioner
appointment/control/governance cannot be made until the relevant authority has received the
Committee's advice — or the Committee has failed to respond within 30 days (or a longer specified
period), in which case the precondition lapses.
