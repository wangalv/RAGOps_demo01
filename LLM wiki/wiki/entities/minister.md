The Minister administering the [[health-services-act-1997]] (Minister for Health, plus related
portfolios per the Administrative Arrangements Order). Holds the Act's directive, approval, and
appointment powers at the political level, distinct from the [[health-secretary]]'s administrative
functions.

## Key powers

- Amend/dissolve/amalgamate/rename local health districts and statutory health corporations, by
  recommendation to the [[governor]] (ss.19–20, 42–43) — always requires a public-interest
  opinion.
- Direct establishment/closure/restriction of services in the public interest (s.32, s.53).
- Approve LHD/statutory health corporation land dealings (s.34, s.55).
- Appoint local health district board members and remove them at will (s.26, s.29).
- Approve standard service contract conditions for visiting medical officers (s.87).
- Hear appeals from visiting practitioners on appointment decisions; appoints
  [[committee-of-review]]s to determine them (s.106, s.108).
- Fix scales of fees for general health services (s.69) and ambulance services (s.67L).
- Determine and defer subsidies, approve loans, require AHO repayment agreements (ss.127–128).
- Direct acquisition of shared services from the Health Secretary or another provider, subject to
  AHO consent (ss.126G–126H).
- Review the Act's policy objectives ~5 years post-assent (s.142).

## Consent constraints

Cannot direct or recognise/declare/network/transfer an [[affiliated-health-organisation]] without
its consent (ss.62, 62A, 62B, 64, 126H) — the recurring theme distinguishing AHOs from
[[local-health-district]]s and [[statutory-health-corporation]]s, which the Minister/Governor
control more directly.
