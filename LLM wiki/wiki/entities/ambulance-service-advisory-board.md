Established directly by [[health-services-act-1997]] (s.67C(1)) — not created by regulation like
a generic [[ch5a-part-2-provision-of-ambulance-services|appointed body]]. Members: the
[[ambulance-service-of-nsw]] chief executive plus 8–12 [[health-secretary]]-appointed members with
expertise in health/financial/business management or clinical paramedic/other health services
(s.67C(2)–(3)). Procedure detail in Schedule 6 (not separately paginated).

## Function

Advises the Health Secretary or an appointed body on the exercise of ambulance-service functions
under Chapter 5A (s.67C(5)), plus any other function the Health Secretary confers (s.67C(6)).
