Constituted by the *Health Administration Act 1982* (not this Act — [[health-services-act-1997]]
refers to it, doesn't create it). Dictionary: "means the Health Administration Corporation
constituted by the Health Administration Act 1982." Functions as a Crown body that can itself
control public hospitals/health services, alongside the [[minister]].

- [[ch2-structure-of-the-public-health-system]] (s.15(d) — a public hospital includes one
