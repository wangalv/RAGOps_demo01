One of the three kinds of [[public-health-organisation]] (see also
[[statutory-health-corporation]], [[affiliated-health-organisation]]). Constituted under s.17,
listed with geographic area in Schedule 1 (15 LHDs, e.g. Sydney, Hunter New England, Western NSW).
Full statutory definition: `wiki/definitions/local-health-district.md`.

## Structure

- A body corporate, but **cannot employ staff directly** — all staff employed via the
  [[nsw-health-service]] (s.22(2)).
- Run by a [[local-health-district-board|board]] (6–13 [[minister]]-appointed members) and a
  chief executive appointed by the board with [[health-secretary]] concurrence (ss.23, 26).
- Primary purposes: relief of sick/injured, promotion/protection/maintenance of community health
  (s.9).

## Key powers

- Combined management arrangements with other LHDs/non-district health organisations, with
  Minister approval (s.30).
- Open/close/restrict services, subject to notification and Health Secretary/Minister direction
  (s.31–32).
- Property acquisition/disposal, contracts, investments, all subject to varying degrees of
  Minister/Health Secretary approval (ss.34–38).
- Make by-laws adopting Health-Secretary model by-laws, with Medical Services Committee
  involvement for visiting-practitioner matters (s.39).
- Delegate functions, except delegation power itself, s.31(2) functions, or by-law making (s.40).

## Key obligations

- Must not exercise functions inconsistently with the Health Secretary (s.28(2)).
- Must notify the Health Secretary before implementing service opening/closing decisions (s.31(3)).
- Must not acquire/dispose of land without Minister approval (s.34(2)).
