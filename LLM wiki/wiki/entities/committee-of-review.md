Ad hoc body the [[minister]] must appoint to determine a [[visiting-practitioner]]'s appeal
against a decision to reduce privileges, refuse re-appointment, or suspend/terminate (s.108).
Dictionary: "Committee" — means a Committee of Review appointed by the Minister under section 108.

## Composition (s.108(2))

- An Australian lawyer of ≥7 years standing (Chairperson).
- [[australian-medical-association-nsw]] nominee (if appellant is a medical practitioner) or
  Australian Dental Association NSW Branch nominee (if a dentist).
- A Minister-appointed consumer-interest member, never a current/former medical practitioner or
  dentist.
- One other Minister-appointed member, experienced in public health system administration (and,
  for competence-based appeals, matching the appellant's profession).

## Powers and procedure

Royal Commissions Act powers/protections apply (s.111); Chairperson decides law/procedure
questions; majority otherwise; Chairperson has a casting vote on a 2–2 split. May refer misconduct
or impairment concerns to the Medical Council or Dental Council of NSW as a Health Practitioner
Regulation National Law complaint (s.112(1A)). Orders take effect from the date made and are
final decisions of the organisation (s.113).
