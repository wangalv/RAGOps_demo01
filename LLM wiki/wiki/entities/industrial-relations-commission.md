External tribunal (not created by this Act) that supplies the [[arbitrator]] for system-wide
visiting-medical-officer fee-for-service/sessional contract disputes under
[[ch8-part-2-service-contracts]] — the Act's one significant delegation of a determination to a
body outside the [[minister]]/[[health-secretary]] chain.

## Role in this Act

- The relevant Minister (administering the *Industrial Relations Act 1996*) appoints a nominated
  IRC judicial member as arbitrator (s.90).
- Arbitrator determinations are final and unreviewable except by appeal to the IRC's **Full
  Bench** (s.95–96) or an application for a declaration of right to the IRC **in Court Session**
  (s.97).
- [[nsw-health-service-senior-executive]] employment disputes are expressly excluded from IRC
  industrial-matter jurisdiction (s.121J) — the opposite treatment from ordinary visiting-officer
  contract disputes.
- General NSW Health Service staff retain ordinary unfair-dismissal rights under the *Industrial
  Relations Act 1996* Part 6, with the IRC required to have regard to s.119 (child/patient
  protection paramountcy) when hearing them (s.121, s.104).
