One of the three kinds of [[public-health-organisation]] (see also [[local-health-district]],
[[affiliated-health-organisation]]). Constituted under s.41, listed in Schedule 2 with a
governance type. Examples: Agency for Clinical Innovation (chief executive governed), Bureau of
Health Information (board governed), Justice Health and Forensic Mental Health Network (specialty
network governed).

## Three governance models

- **Board governed** ([[health-corporation-board]]) — CE (ex officio) + 5–11 Minister-appointed
  members; board controls affairs, subject to Minister direction except re reports to the
  Minister (ss.46–52).
- **Chief executive governed** — CE appointed by [[health-secretary]], manages/controls directly;
  Minister may establish an advisory council (ss.52A–52E).
- **Specialty network governed** — its own board, run under the local-health-district-board rules
  (Ch.3 Pt 2 Div 2) applied by modification (ss.52F–52G).

Like a local health district, **cannot employ staff directly** — relies on the
[[nsw-health-service]] (s.45(2)).

## Key powers

- Joint/assisted management with other statutory health corporations, Minister approval needed
  (s.53A).
- Property, contracts, investments, by-laws — parallel to [[local-health-district]]'s Chapter 3
  Part 3 powers, but gated through a "relevant authority" that varies by governance type: Minister
  for board-governed, Health Secretary for chief-executive/specialty-network-governed (ss.55–61).
