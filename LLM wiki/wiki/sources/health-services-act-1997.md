The primary statute regulating the NSW public health system. Repealed the *Area Health Services
Act 1986* and *Public Hospitals Act 1929*. Version ingested: current for 10 June 2026 to date
(accessed 4 September 2026). See [[health-services-act-1997-overview]] for full structure.

## Purpose (s.4 objects)

- Establish local health districts to deliver health services (recognisable as health networks
  under the National Health Reform Agreement).
- Constitute statutory health corporations to deliver services on a non-area basis.
- Recognise affiliated health organisations (non-government providers) as part of the public
  health system.
- Re-affirm the Medicare Principles and Commitments and facilitate fee collection consistent
  with them.
- Regulate visiting medical officer contracts.
- Facilitate staff transfers within the public health system.
- Require disclosure of serious sex/violence offence charges and convictions by visiting
  practitioners and staff, and specify consequences.
- Fund public health organisations and enable efficient administration (inquiries, inspections,
  transfers).

## Key themes

- **Three-tier public health system**: [[local-health-district]], [[statutory-health-corporation]],
  [[affiliated-health-organisation]] — see [[public-health-organisation]].
- **Governance layering**: [[minister]] → [[health-secretary]] → chief executives/boards of each
  public health organisation, with delegable functions throughout.
- **Employment**: nearly all public health system staff are employed centrally in the
  [[nsw-health-service]], not by the individual organisations they work for — see Chapter 9.
- **Visiting practitioners**: a distinct, contract-based (not employment-based) regime for
  doctors/dentists working in public hospitals — Chapter 8.
- **Ambulance services**: added later (2006/2014) as Chapter 5A, with its own fee-charging and
  debt-recovery regime distinct from the rest of the Act.
- **Safeguarding**: mandatory disclosure and termination/disciplinary provisions for serious
  sex/violence offences and professional misconduct findings, applied in parallel to visiting
  practitioners (Ch 8 Pt 3) and staff (Ch 9 Pt 2).

## Structure

11 Chapters (1–11, plus 5A), ~453 numbered provisions, 9 Schedules, and a Dictionary of ~62
defined terms. Full breakdown: [[health-services-act-1997-overview]].

## Scope note on this ingest

Full deep ingest per user request: every Part/Chapter has a page with a section table, every
Dictionary term has a definition page, and obligations/powers matrices span the whole Act. The 9
Schedules (mostly named lists — local health districts, statutory health corporations,
affiliated health organisations — and board procedure/transitional boilerplate) are described in
the overview but not given individual pages, since they're reference data rather than narrative
provisions; this deviates from doing literally everything and is noted here rather than silently
decided.
