## Notes

- This is not exhaustive of every subsection-level "must" in the Act (~453 provisions); it
  captures the operative obligations flagged while drafting each Part page. Deep-dive a specific
  Part (per CLAUDE.md's "Deep-dive on a specific section" workflow) to extract finer-grained
  obligations if needed.
- Several "must" provisions are procedural preconditions within larger power-grants (e.g. the
  Medical Services Committee advice requirement at s.39(6), s.60(6), s.63(3), s.140(6)) rather
  than free-standing duties — these are noted on the relevant Part pages
  ([[ch3-part-3-functions]], [[ch4-part-3-functions]], [[ch5-affiliated-health-organisations]],
  [[ch11-miscellaneous]]) rather than duplicated here.
